Privacy policy

This policy describes the personal OAuth client named Rclone Personal Google Drive. It is a connection for the account owner's local rclone application, rather than a hosted file-processing service.

Google data and permissions

The client requests Google Drive access to list folders and file metadata, download files the owner accesses, and upload, update, move, or delete files as instructed by the owner. The requested Drive permission covers existing files and folders in the authorized account.

Where data is processed

Rclone runs on the owner's computer. File transfers take place between that computer and Google's services. Authorization tokens and downloaded file content are stored locally. Local diagnostic logs may contain filenames and transfer information.

This information website does not receive authorization tokens or Drive files. It contains no forms, advertising, tracking scripts, or file upload controls. Its hosting provider may retain technical access logs associated with requests to this website.

Use and sharing

Google data is used to provide the owner's requested file-access and file-management functions. This client does not sell Google data or use it for advertising or model training. Other applications the owner chooses to use with mounted files, including AI assistants, are separate services and have their own data-handling policies.

Retention and removal

Downloaded content is kept in a local cache according to the owner's settings. Clean cached copies can be removed without deleting the files in Google Drive. Unuploaded changes must be preserved until their upload succeeds. File deletions performed through the mounted Drive affect the cloud files.

The owner can stop the connection and revoke its authorization in Google Account connections. Local tokens, cached content, and diagnostic logs can then be removed from the computer. Revoking access does not itself delete files held in Google Drive.

Contact

Questions about this personal client can be directed to the support email shown on its Google consent screen.
