Personal desktop connection

Rclone Personal Google Drive

This personal OAuth client connects its owner's computer to Google Drive using the open-source rclone application.

The connection lets the owner browse Drive folders and open, create, edit, and delete files through their computer's filesystem. File contents are fetched as needed, and local changes are uploaded to Google Drive.

This website provides app information. It does not provide a file browser, collect Google credentials, or receive Drive files.

Account access

Google authorization happens on Google's sign-in pages. The connection requests Drive access so it can work with existing folders and files. The client is intended for its owner's personal use.

Read the privacy policy for details about permissions, local caching, and revoking access.

Support contact: the email displayed on the client's Google consent screen.
